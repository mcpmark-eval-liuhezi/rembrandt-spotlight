## Night Watch citation (verified against the Rijksmuseum collection record)

- Inventory number: **SK-C-5**
- Title: Het korporaalschap van kapitein Frans Banninck Cocq en luitenant Willem van Ruytenburch, bekend als de 'Nachtwacht'
- Completed: **1642**
- Confirmation: the object number SK-C-5 resolves to this painting in the museum's official collection record, dated 1642.

- Timeline spans 1626–1665.
