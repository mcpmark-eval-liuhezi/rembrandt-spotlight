Chronological survey of Rembrandt van Rijn works from the Rijksmuseum collection, compiled for the museum-education blog's "Dutch Masters" series.

**Sources:** Rijksmuseum collection records (Superservice / Linked Art queries). Each entry's inventory number and date were checked against the museum's official collection record.
