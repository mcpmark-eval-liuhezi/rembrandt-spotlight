# Rembrandt Spotlight

Content hub for the museum-education blog's **Dutch Masters** series.

## Current feature: Rembrandt collection timeline

A chronological timeline of 10 works by Rembrandt van Rijn from the Rijksmuseum collection, each with title, date, and inventory number:

- Night Watch — SK-C-5, completed 1642 (verified against the museum's official collection record)
- The Stone Bridge — SK-A-1935, 1638
- The Jewish Bride — SK-C-216, 1665
- The Anatomy Lesson of Dr. Nicolaes Tulp — SK-C-6, 1662
- And 6 more works spanning 1626–1665

See [`rembrandt-timeline.md`](rembrandt-timeline.md) for the full timeline.
